# Winning by Design: SPICED Methodology Reference

## Overview

The SPICED framework is a qualification and discovery methodology developed by Winning by Design (WbD) that helps sales professionals uncover the complete buying context. Unlike traditional qualification frameworks that focus on seller needs, SPICED centers on understanding the buyer's journey.

---

## The SPICED Framework

### S - Situation

**Definition:** The current state of the prospect's business, including their existing processes, tools, and market position.

**Key Questions to Uncover:**
- "Can you walk me through how you currently handle [process]?"
- "What tools/systems are you using today?"
- "How long have you been doing it this way?"
- "How many people are involved in this process?"

**Best Practice Talk Tracks:**
- "Before we dive into solutions, I'd love to understand your current setup..."
- "Help me understand your world today..."
- "Paint me a picture of how things work right now..."

**Scoring Criteria (1-5):**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | No understanding of current state established |
| 2 | Basic/surface-level understanding only |
| 3 | Moderate understanding of key processes |
| 4 | Good grasp of current tools, processes, and team structure |
| 5 | Deep understanding including metrics, workflows, and context |

---

### P - Pain

**Definition:** The specific problems, challenges, or frustrations the prospect is experiencing with their current situation.

**Key Questions to Uncover:**
- "What's not working well with your current approach?"
- "What are the biggest challenges you're facing?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would you change?"
- "What keeps you up at night about [area]?"

**Best Practice Talk Tracks:**
- "It sounds like you're experiencing..."
- "So the main challenge is..."
- "Let me make sure I understand the problem..."

**Pain Categories:**
1. **Process Pain** - Inefficient workflows, manual tasks
2. **People Pain** - Team struggles, skill gaps, turnover
3. **Technology Pain** - Tool limitations, integration issues
4. **Financial Pain** - Revenue loss, cost overruns
5. **Strategic Pain** - Competitive pressure, market changes

**Scoring Criteria (1-5):**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | No pain identified or discussed |
| 2 | Vague or assumed pain, not validated |
| 3 | One clear pain point identified |
| 4 | Multiple pain points with some depth |
| 5 | Deep understanding of interconnected pain points with quantification |

---

### I - Impact

**Definition:** The measurable business consequences of the pain - both the cost of inaction and the value of solving the problem.

**Key Questions to Uncover:**
- "How is this impacting your business/team?"
- "What does this cost you in terms of [time/money/resources]?"
- "If this problem continues, what happens?"
- "What would solving this mean for you personally/professionally?"

**Impact Quantification Framework:**
1. **Time Impact** - Hours lost per week/month
2. **Revenue Impact** - Lost deals, slower sales cycles
3. **Cost Impact** - Wasted resources, inefficiencies
4. **Risk Impact** - Compliance, competitive threats
5. **Personal Impact** - Career, stress, reputation

**Best Practice Talk Tracks:**
- "Help me understand the magnitude of this..."
- "What's the downstream effect of this problem?"
- "If we were to quantify this..."

**Scoring Criteria (1-5):**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | No impact discussed |
| 2 | Impact mentioned but not quantified |
| 3 | Basic impact understood with some context |
| 4 | Clear business impact with some quantification |
| 5 | Fully quantified ROI with personal and business impact |

---

### C - Critical Event

**Definition:** A specific event, deadline, or trigger that creates urgency and drives the timeline for making a decision.

**Key Questions to Uncover:**
- "Is there a specific date or event driving this?"
- "What happens if you don't solve this by [date]?"
- "What's prompting you to look at this now?"
- "What would cause this project to be delayed or accelerated?"

**Types of Critical Events:**
1. **External Events** - Regulatory deadlines, market changes, competitive moves
2. **Internal Events** - Board meetings, fiscal year end, leadership changes
3. **Project Events** - Go-live dates, product launches, expansions
4. **Personal Events** - Performance reviews, role changes
5. **Trigger Events** - Budget cycles, strategic initiatives

**Best Practice Talk Tracks:**
- "What's driving the timing on this?"
- "Is there a specific deadline we're working towards?"
- "What happens if this extends past [timeframe]?"

**Scoring Criteria (1-5):**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | No timeline or urgency established |
| 2 | Vague timeline ("sometime this year") |
| 3 | General timeline with soft deadline |
| 4 | Clear critical event with specific date |
| 5 | Compelling critical event with consequences for missing it |

---

### E - Expected Decision (Process)

**Definition:** Understanding how the decision will be made, who is involved, and what the evaluation process looks like.

**Key Questions to Uncover:**
- "Who else is involved in this decision?"
- "Walk me through your typical evaluation process..."
- "What does your approval process look like?"
- "Who has final sign-off authority?"

**Decision Process Elements:**
1. **Decision Makers** - Who has authority?
2. **Influencers** - Who shapes the decision?
3. **Champions** - Who advocates internally?
4. **Blockers** - Who might resist?
5. **Process Steps** - What stages must be completed?

**MEDDIC Integration:**
- **M**etrics (covered in Impact)
- **E**conomic Buyer (key decision maker)
- **D**ecision Criteria (covered in next section)
- **D**ecision Process (this section)
- **I**dentify Pain (covered in Pain)
- **C**hampion (internal advocate)

**Scoring Criteria (1-5):**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | No understanding of decision process |
| 2 | Know only one contact, unclear authority |
| 3 | Basic understanding of stakeholders |
| 4 | Clear map of decision makers and process |
| 5 | Full stakeholder map with champion and clear next steps |

---

### D - Decision Criteria

**Definition:** The specific requirements, priorities, and evaluation metrics the prospect will use to make their decision.

**Key Questions to Uncover:**
- "What are the must-have requirements?"
- "How will you evaluate potential solutions?"
- "What does success look like for this project?"
- "What would make you choose one solution over another?"

**Decision Criteria Categories:**
1. **Technical Requirements** - Features, integrations, security
2. **Business Requirements** - ROI, time to value
3. **Vendor Requirements** - Support, reputation, stability
4. **Implementation Requirements** - Timeline, resources needed
5. **Risk Requirements** - Compliance, change management

**Best Practice Talk Tracks:**
- "If we were to move forward, what criteria would you use to evaluate..."
- "What's on your must-have vs nice-to-have list?"
- "How will you know if this is successful?"

**Scoring Criteria (1-5):**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | No criteria discussed |
| 2 | Assumed or generic criteria |
| 3 | Some requirements identified |
| 4 | Clear prioritized list of criteria |
| 5 | Detailed success criteria aligned with our strengths |

## Call Structure Best Practices

### Opening (First 5 Minutes)

**Goals:**
- Build rapport
- Set agenda
- Confirm time

**Talk Track:**
> "Thanks for taking the time today. I've set aside [X] minutes - does that still work? Great. What I'd like to do is start by understanding your current situation, and then we can explore if there's a fit. Does that work for you?"

### Discovery (Core of Call)

**Goals:**
- Uncover all SPICED elements
- Listen more than talk (aim for 60/40 ratio)
- Ask follow-up questions
- Take detailed notes

**Transition Phrases:**
- "That's really helpful context. Can I dig deeper on..."
- "I want to make sure I understand..."
- "Building on that..."

### Closing (Final 5 Minutes)

**Goals:**
- Summarize understanding
- Confirm next steps
- Get commitment

**Talk Track:**
> "Let me summarize what I heard... [recap SPICED]. Does that accurately capture things? Based on what you've shared, I think the logical next step would be... What do you think?"

## Common Coaching Opportunities

### 1. Surface-Level Discovery
**Issue:** Rep asks questions but doesn't go deep enough
**Coaching:** Use the "5 Whys" technique - keep asking "why" to uncover root causes

### 2. Premature Pitching
**Issue:** Rep jumps to solution before understanding problem
**Coaching:** Create "no pitch zones" - the first half of discovery calls should be questions only

### 3. Missing Stakeholders
**Issue:** Rep sells to a single contact without mapping the org
**Coaching:** Always ask "Who else should be involved in this conversation?"

### 4. No Quantified Impact
**Issue:** Pain is identified but not measured
**Coaching:** Use "How much is that costing you?" and "What would fixing that be worth?"

### 5. Weak Next Steps
**Issue:** Calls end without clear commitments
**Coaching:** Always propose a specific next step with date/time before ending

### 6. One-Way Conversation
**Issue:** Rep talks more than listens
**Coaching:** Track talk-time ratios; aim for prospect speaking 60%+ of the time

### 7. Feature Dumping
**Issue:** Rep lists features instead of connecting to pain
**Coaching:** Every feature mentioned should connect back to a stated pain point

### 8. Missing Critical Event
**Issue:** No urgency established
**Coaching:** Always understand "Why now?" and "What happens if you don't act?"

## Team Benchmarking Guidelines

### SPICED Score Targets by Role

| Role | Target Avg Score |
|------|-----------------|
| SDR/BDR | 3.0+ per element |
| AE (Early Stage) | 3.5+ per element |
| AE (Enterprise) | 4.0+ per element |
| Sales Leader | 4.5+ per element |

### Key Performance Indicators

1. **Discovery Depth Score** - Average of all SPICED elements
2. **Improvement Velocity** - Week-over-week score changes
3. **Consistency** - Standard deviation across calls
4. **Gap Frequency** - How often each element is missed
5. **Conversion Correlation** - SPICED scores vs win rates

### Coaching Frequency

- **Weekly:** Review 1-2 calls per rep
- **Monthly:** Trend analysis and goal setting
- **Quarterly:** Comprehensive skill assessment

## WbD Revenue Architecture Alignment

### The Bowtie Model

SPICED discovery feeds into the broader revenue architecture:

```
AWARENESS → EDUCATION → SELECTION → ONBOARDING → IMPACT → GROWTH
                          ↑
                     SPICED Discovery
```

### Growth Levers

1. **Volume** - More opportunities
2. **Velocity** - Faster cycles
3. **Value** - Larger deals
4. **Conversion** - Higher win rates

Better SPICED execution improves all four levers by:
- Creating urgency (velocity)
- Establishing value (value/conversion)
- Building champions (velocity/conversion)
- Reducing churn (impact/growth)

## Quick Reference Card

### SPICED Summary

| Element | Core Question | Red Flag |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Situation | "How do things work today?" | No current state understanding |
| Pain | "What's not working?" | No explicit problems stated |
| Impact | "What does this cost you?" | No quantification |
| Critical Event | "What's driving the timing?" | No urgency or deadline |
| Expected Decision | "Who decides and how?" | Single-threaded contact |
| Decision Criteria | "How will you choose?" | Unclear requirements |

### Talk Time Guidelines

- **Discovery Calls:** Rep 30-40% / Prospect 60-70%
- **Demo Calls:** Rep 50-60% / Prospect 40-50%
- **Negotiation:** Rep 40-50% / Prospect 50-60%
